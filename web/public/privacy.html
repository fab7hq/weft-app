Privacy

Weft on the web lets you see and answer the coding agents running in Weft on your own machine. The screens, the keys you press and the notifications go between your device and your machine. They do not pass through Fab7.

What Fab7 keeps

  • your GitHub id and login
  • a hash of your session cookie, ended after 30 days
  • each machine's name, token hash and last address

What Fab7 never sees or keeps

  • screens, typing, prompts and records
  • notifications · your GitHub token · device tokens

Where it lives

Cloudflare Worker and D1; traffic is decrypted at Cloudflare's edge; Fab7 makes no end-to-end claim for it.

Leaving

Sign out · sign out everywhere · Delete my account, in Settings. Removing a device happens on your machine, in Weft: W → R.
