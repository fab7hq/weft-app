Privacy

Weft on the web lets you see and answer the coding agents running in Weft on your own Mac. The screens, the keys you press and the notifications go between your device and your Mac. They do not pass through Fab7.

What Fab7 keeps

  • Your GitHub account's numeric id and login, read once when you sign in.
  • A record of each session you are signed in with: the SHA-256 of its cookie and when it ends, 30 days after you sign in.
  • Each Mac you add: the name it gives itself, the SHA-256 of its own token, and the address it last reported, with when.

What Fab7 never sees or keeps

  • Your agents' screens, what you type, the prompts Weft sends, and RingFrame's records.
  • Notifications: your Mac sends them, encrypted to your device, through Apple's or Google's push service.
  • Your GitHub token: it is used to read who you are and then dropped.
  • The token that lets a device into your Mac: your Mac makes it and keeps only its hash.

Where it lives

On Cloudflare (a Worker and a D1 database). Your Mac is reached through a Cloudflare tunnel, where traffic is decrypted at Cloudflare's edge; Fab7 makes no end-to-end claim for it.

Leaving

  • Sign out ends this session. Sign out everywhere ends all of them.
  • Delete my account, in Settings, deletes your account, your sessions and your Macs at once.
  • Removing a device happens on your Mac, in Weft: W → R.
